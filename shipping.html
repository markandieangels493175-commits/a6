Shipping Policy

Clear information about how Jacket Compass Way operates.

Availability and dispatch

No jacket is immediately available unless confirmed during an order. Dispatch estimates, carrier choices, insurance and charges are provided before payment. Small-batch timelines may vary.

Address and tracking

Provide a complete delivery address. Tracking is sent where available after dispatch. Carrier scans and estimated dates can change and do not guarantee a specific appointment.

International delivery

International parcels may incur tax, duty, customs processing or carrier fees. Responsibility for these charges will be explained before purchase where known; border processing may extend delivery time.

Transit problems

Inspect the parcel promptly. If damaged, retain packaging, photograph the condition and email hello@jacketcompassway.com as soon as practical. We will review evidence and carrier processes without limiting statutory rights.

Questions may be sent to hello@jacketcompassway.com or by post to 3638 Westbourne Grove, London, UK, W2 5SH, GB.
