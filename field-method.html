FIELD METHOD / 01

Read the route
before the jacket.

Our five-coordinate method connects environment, activity, layers, fit and maintenance.

FIVE COORDINATES

A complete outerwear brief.

  1. 01

    Exposure

    Record temperature range, wind, precipitation, duration and shelter.

  2. 02

    Activity

    Match protection and venting to how much heat and moisture movement creates.

  3. 03

    Layers

    Plan the base and midlayers before judging jacket size or internal volume.

  4. 04

    Movement

    Test reach, bend, closure access and hem position in realistic use.

  5. 05

    Service

    Understand cleaning, drying, reproofing and repair access before purchase.
